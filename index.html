About

01 — Profile

Design is my passion — and I believe a designer's mindset is all that's needed to design anything.

Aesthetics and style change over time, but design thinking is about solving problems with an open, curious mind. Through this approach I've designed multi-million dollar homes, offices for international companies, a product collaboration with a celebrity, social campaigns for Chase Bank, and many other diverse projects.

Design is my passion — and I believe a designer's mindset is all that's needed to design anything.

Aesthetics and style change over time, but design thinking is about solving problems with an open, curious mind. As a creator, I strive to maintain this perspective in all of my work. Through it, I've had the opportunity to design multi-million dollar homes, offices for international companies, a product collaboration with a celebrity, social media ads for Chase Bank, and many other diverse projects.

Alongside design, I've been deeply interested in color my whole life. I can't remember a time I wasn't studying it. Through continued research I've become an expert in the way color communicates and how people interact with it. Color is everything — and nothing exists unless it is first illuminated by light.
